THE STORY

Four Rings. One Encounter. A Universe Kevin Never Knew Existed.

Kevin's life is about to become much bigger, stranger and more dangerous than he could possibly imagine.

Kevin has no reason to believe that his ordinary life is connected to anything beyond the world he knows.

Then come the four rings.

What follows draws Kevin into contact with Nivek and opens the door to extraordinary technology, an alien spacecraft and a civilisation whose existence changes everything Kevin thought he understood about the universe.

But discovery brings danger with it. Kevin soon finds himself caught up in events far beyond his experience, where friendship and trust may matter every bit as much as understanding the impossible things happening around him.

AT THE HEART OF THE STORY

The Four Rings

They are more than objects. They are the beginning of Kevin's connection to a world beyond his own.

The four rings lie at the centre of Kevin's first extraordinary adventure.

Through them, the familiar begins to give way to something much larger: new technology, new worlds and the realisation that humanity is far from alone.

For Kevin, however, the most important discovery is not simply that another civilisation exists. It is that the people he encounters are capable of friendship, loyalty and courage despite everything that separates their worlds.

TWO WORLDS MEET

Kevin and Nivek

At the centre of The Four Rings is the relationship between Kevin and Nivek.

They come from different worlds and begin with very different understandings of what is happening around them.

Yet as Kevin is drawn deeper into Nivek's world, their connection becomes increasingly important. What begins with the extraordinary develops into something much more recognisable: trust, friendship and the willingness to help someone when they need you.

It is a relationship that will matter far beyond the events of the first book.

WHY THIS STORY MATTERS

The First Step Into a Much Larger Universe

The Four Rings begins as a story of discovery, but it becomes something larger. Kevin learns that encountering another world means confronting new dangers, questioning old assumptions and deciding who deserves his trust.
