WHO IS GROUP CAPTAIN FRANKS?

The Man Responsible for Securing the Base

Group Captain Franks is trained to respond to danger quickly. When impossible technology appears inside a secure Air Force facility, he does not have the luxury of assuming it is harmless.

To Franks, Nivek and Leinad are unknown aliens with access to a Power Cell capable of changing the balance of power on Earth. Kevin may know what is truly at stake, but convincing an armed commander in the middle of a crisis is another matter.

Franks is not cruel. He is doing the job he believes will keep everyone safe. Unfortunately, his determination to control the situation places him directly in the path of a damaged ship trying desperately to escape.

A Few Things to Know

ROLE

Air Force Commander

Franks is responsible for securing the base and responding when alien technology threatens to escape his control.

NATURE

Decisive and Suspicious

He makes rapid decisions under pressure and will not lower his guard simply because a twelve-year-old asks him to.

PRIORITY

Contain the Threat

Franks intends to keep the aliens, their ship and the Power Cell secured until he understands exactly what they can do.

CONFLICT

Orders Before Explanations

Kevin needs time to explain. Franks believes waiting could place the base—and the wider world—in even greater danger.
