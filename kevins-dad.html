WHO IS KEVIN'S DAD?

The Man Behind the Mystery

Kevin has been told that his dad died nine years earlier when his car struck a wall. Kevin refuses to accept the official story—and when Dad reappears, that impossible belief begins to look like the truth.

Dad is protective without treating Kevin as helpless. A steady hand on his shoulder, a rough headlock or a quiet “I'm proud of you” can give Kevin the courage to face whatever comes next.

Their reunion gives Kevin something he has wanted for most of his life, but it also raises larger questions. Where has Dad been, why was Kevin told he had died, and how much of their family story is true?
