WHO IS PROFESSOR SHARPE?

The Scientist Seeking the Truth

Professor Sharpe approaches the alien Power Cell as both a scientific mystery and a discovery capable of transforming the world. He wants evidence, explanations and answers that can be tested.

The greatest source of those answers is Leinad. Professor Sharpe believes the alien understands the Power Cell's origin, purpose and true capabilities, but Leinad is unwilling to cooperate.

Curiosity makes Professor Sharpe persistent, but the investigation carries dangers no ordinary laboratory was designed to contain. The closer he gets to the truth, the greater the consequences become.
