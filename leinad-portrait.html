WHO IS LEINAD?

The Alien Who Knows Too Much

Leinad understands the Power Cell and the danger surrounding it better than almost anyone. His determination to reach it brings alien technology, military force and Earth's future into collision.

His history with Nivek makes their conflict personal. Nivek remembers a sequence in which Leinad died, but the broken timeline gives both of them another chance to make a different choice.

Leinad is not dangerous because he knows nothing. He is dangerous because he knows exactly what the Power Cell can do—and believes his own purpose justifies the risks he asks everyone else to face.
