WHO IS BOLESH?

The Creature Who Bargains with Time

Bolesh is an enormous spider-like creature who has lived inside the mountain for longer than anyone can remember. Her age has weakened her body, but not her intelligence, authority or hunger.

She can distort the passage of time by plucking the strands of her web, allowing Kevin and Nivek to move between moments. The gift is powerful, unstable and limited by Bolesh's failing strength.

Beneath Bolesh's threats is an ancient loneliness. Her bargain is not simply about letting travellers pass. She wants them to find a male of her kind before age leaves her species without a future.

AT A GLANCE

A Few Things to Know

ROLE

Keeper of the Mountain

Bolesh controls the paths through her lair and decides whether travellers pass, bargain or become food.

NATURE

Ancient and Lonely

Time has left Bolesh isolated, weakening her body while sharpening her awareness of everything she may lose.

ABILITY

Distorting Time

By using her web, Bolesh can slow time until one minute outside becomes far longer for those inside her influence.

DEEPEST NEED

A Future for Her Kind

Bolesh asks the travellers to bring back a male before age and isolation end her chance to continue her species.
