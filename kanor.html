WHO IS KANOR?

The Scientist Behind the Mystery

Kanor is a highly intelligent warrior/scientist whose careful manner makes him difficult to read. He observes first, speaks precisely and rarely explains more than he believes is necessary.

His knowledge of power stations on Dra-Conia surrounding Kevin's journey makes him invaluable. It also means he often knows more about Kevin's situation than he is willing to reveal.

Kanor's formal habit of addressing Kevin as “Lord” leaves Kevin unsure whether he has been honoured, promoted or placed in even greater danger. Behind the title, however, Kanor recognises something important in him.
