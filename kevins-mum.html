WHO IS KEVIN'S MUM?

The Voice Kevin Carries with Him

Kevin's mum loves him fiercely, even when she cannot accept his belief that his dad may still be alive. She wants to protect her son from danger, disappointment and a grief he is too young to carry.

She calls him “KK,” brushes the hair from his eyes and gives him the ordinary moments he misses most when he is far from home. In Kevin's memories, those small gestures become a source of courage.

Mum's presence reaches far beyond the moments she shares directly with Kevin. Whenever he is frightened, lost or unsure who to trust, the thought of returning to her gives him a reason to keep going.
