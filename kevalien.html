AT THE HEART OF THE TRILOGY

The Universe May Be Vast. The Choices Are Personal.

Beneath the spacecraft, alien worlds and extraordinary discoveries, Ke'Valien is a story about people: friendship, loyalty, courage and the decisions we make when there is no easy answer.

THE JOURNEY

Discovery. Conflict. Choice.

The Four Rings opens Kevin's eyes to a universe he never knew existed.

The Dra-Conian War forces him to face what happens when that universe is threatened.

He Must Choose Who He Becomes brings Kevin to the decision towards which the entire journey has been leading.

Across all three books, the scale of the adventure grows, but the most important questions remain deeply personal: Who can Kevin trust? What is worth fighting for? And when everything changes, who does he want to be?
