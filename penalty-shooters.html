WHO ARE THE PENALTY SHOOTERS?

Five Shots Between Jake and Victory

Mason Clarke, Reece Morgan, Dylan Harper, Tyler Bennett and Connor Hayes are five school footballers with one useful thing in common: every one of them scored in the previous year's penalty shoot-out against Kingsbrook Academy.

That makes them exactly the wrong five people for Jake to face when his latest challenge puts him in goal. To win, Jake has to save at least three of their five penalties at lunchtime, with Mr Briggs supervising.

Alex studies Ryan's video footage and spots patterns in the boys' penalties. Jake manages to adapt, but saves only Reece's and Connor's shots. He loses the challenge 3–2—and earns three football practices serving drinks and oranges.
