WHO IS FINN?

The Friend Who Believes Him

Finn is one of Kevin's friends on Earth. He is practical enough to question Kevin's wildest claims, but loyal enough to listen when Kevin needs him most.

When Kevin shows him the truth, Finn does not pretend to understand every piece of alien technology. He accepts the evidence in front of him and concentrates on what his friend needs.

Finn's importance comes from a simple choice: he believes Kevin. When Kevin leaves to search for Nivek and the truth about his parents, Finn promises to explain everything to Gran if he does not return.

ROLE

Kevin's Friend

Finn provides a link to Kevin's ordinary life when almost nothing about Kevin's world feels ordinary anymore.

NATURE

Practical and Loyal

Finn asks sensible questions, but he does not abandon Kevin simply because the answers sound unbelievable.

IMPORTANT PROMISE

Look After Gran

Kevin trusts Finn to tell Gran the truth and look after her if his journey into space does not bring him home.

STRENGTH

Believing the Impossible

Finn changes his mind when the evidence changes, even when accepting the truth turns his whole world upside down.
